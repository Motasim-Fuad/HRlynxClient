# HRlynx

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/screenshots/icon.png" width="96" alt="HRlynx icon" />
</p>

HRlynx is an AI-powered HR companion for Android and iOS. It helps HR professionals stay updated with daily news and get practical guidance from AI HR personas.

Built with **Flutter** and **GetX**. Subscriptions use **RevenueCat** on both **Google Play** and the **Apple App Store**.

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lynxova.hrlnyx) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hrlynx/id6752120098)

---

## Features

- Email, Google, and Apple sign in
- HR persona selection
- AI chat (text and voice) with chat history
- Daily HR QuickScan™ news
- Push notifications
- Profile and account management
- In-app subscription on Apple and Google
